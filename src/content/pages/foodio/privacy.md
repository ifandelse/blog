**Last updated: July 21, 2026**

Foodio is a personal food and symptom diary operated by James Cowart. It lets a household member record what they ate and any symptoms they experienced, by text message or by phone call. This policy explains what information Foodio collects and how it is used.

## Information we collect

Foodio collects only what it needs to keep your diary:

- Your mobile phone number.
- Your first name.
- The diary content you provide yourself: the foods and symptoms you describe when you reply to a text or speak during a call, along with the date and time of each entry.

Foodio does not collect your location, your contacts, or any information beyond what you choose to share when logging an entry.

## How we use your information

Your information is used for one purpose: to record your diary entries and make them available to you (and anyone you have asked to help review them, such as a family member or your doctor). Foodio does not use your information for advertising or marketing of any kind.

## Text messaging and consent

You receive Foodio text messages only after you have agreed to them. Your mobile number and your consent to receive messages are never sold, rented, or shared with third parties, and are never shared with anyone for their own marketing.

Message frequency is approximately one message per day. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages, and HELP for help.

## Service providers

Foodio relies on a small number of service providers to function: a telephony provider that delivers the text messages and connects the phone calls, and an AI provider that transcribes calls and turns your words into structured diary entries. These providers process your information solely to operate Foodio on our behalf, and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes or marketing.

## Data retention and your control

Your diary entries are kept so the diary remains useful over time. You can ask to have your information deleted at any time by contacting us. Replying STOP stops all further messages.

## Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests to delete your information, can be sent to jim@ifandelse.com.
